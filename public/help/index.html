Region and redeem guide

South Africa is the only African country with an official PlayStation Store, so you need a South African (ZAR) PSN account. A code bought for another region will not redeem.

Check my account region

  1. On console: Settings → Users and Accounts → Account → Account Information → Region/Country.
  2. In a browser: sign in to account.sonyentertainmentnetwork.com and open your profile details.
  3. It must say South Africa.

I don't have a South African account

Create a new PSN account and choose South Africa as the country. Region cannot be changed later on an existing account, so a new account is the safe route. Games and funds on your current account do not transfer. (Demo placeholder: replace with the reviewed step-by-step guide and screenshots.)

Redeem my code

  • PS5: PlayStation Store → ... → Redeem Codes.
  • PS4: PlayStation Store → Redeem Codes.
  • Phone / browser: PlayStation Store → Redeem Code.
