Help

PlayStation codes from GameLock are for South African accounts. South Africa is the only African country with an official PlayStation Store.

Check your PSN account region
  1. On PS5, open Settings → Users and Accounts → Account → Account Information.
  2. Look at Country/Region. It must say South Africa.
  3. If it shows another country, the card will not redeem. Create a South African account instead.
screenshot · PS5 account information
Create a South African PSN account
  1. Choose Create an account on your console or on the PlayStation website.
  2. Set Country/Region to South Africa. It cannot be changed later.
  3. Verify your email, then sign in on your console.
Redeem your code
  1. PS5: open PlayStation Store, select ··· at the top, then Redeem code.
  2. Phone: open the PlayStation App, go to Store, then Redeem code.
  3. Enter the code exactly as shown. The credit goes to your wallet.
screenshot · redeem code screen
Refunds
  1. Codes are digital and non-refundable once delivered.
  2. If you paid and no code arrived, your order page shows a reference. Send it to support and we will resend the code or refund you.
